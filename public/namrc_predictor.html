This fitted model was trained on all 145 quality-screened labeled records. Its output for a training record is an in-sample fitted estimate, not the out-of-fold validation prediction shown on the validation dashboard. Exploratory grouped-CV metrics are reported separately.

Research integrity and scope

The portable prediction is calculated as intercept + Σ coefficient × ((feature − training mean) / training scale). Input values are checked against observed training ranges; out-of-range values trigger extrapolation warnings, not automatic rejection. Missing, nonfinite and physically invalid inputs are blocked. Even values individually inside range can form an unrealistic combination. No wear threshold, tool-replacement recommendation, safety certification, causal effect, or future-run forecast is implied. Negative numerical predictions, if produced, are displayed as model limitations rather than silently modified.
