Research methodology and interpretation

Source: NASA Milling experimental dataset. The prediction JSON contains out-of-fold estimates from five-fold GroupKFold validation grouped by experimental case. These values are historical validation predictions, not outputs from a fitted deployable inference model. Sensor summaries describe full runs; therefore this module does not demonstrate live monitoring, early-warning prediction, or causal effects of changing feed, depth of cut, or material. Experiment 18 was provisionally excluded from model training because of extreme sensor-summary values. Wear units and the anomalous raw signal require verification against the NASA archive documentation. The model may output negative wear estimates, which are displayed unchanged to preserve validation fidelity.

Data provenance and source checksum are available in model_results.json. Explore the full model validation in the NAMRC dashboard.
